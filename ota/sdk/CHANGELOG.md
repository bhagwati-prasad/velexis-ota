# Changelog of `@velexis/ota-sdk`

Every change that a consumer of the SDK can notice is recorded here by hand, newest first ([OADR-0016](../docs/adr/0016-sdk-packaging-and-type-contract.md) point 8). The SDK follows [semantic versioning](https://semver.org/): a change that the contract test would reject is a major version, new methods and options are minor, fixes are patches. Versions `0.x` live only in the workspace (`workspace:*` in the storefront and the OTA admin); `1.0.0` is the first npm release (M6). `VERSION` in `src/version.js` always equals `version` in `package.json` (a unit test fails otherwise), so a release changes both and adds its section here.

A method that will be removed is marked `@deprecated` in its JSDoc for at least one minor release first, and the deprecation is listed here.

## 0.1.0 (unreleased, workspace only)

### Added

- The MVP additions of `console` and `storefront` (SDK-012, `docs/extensions.md` §3.1–§3.3): `console.settings` with `markupRules` and `feeRules` (`list`, `create`, `get`, `update`, `delete`), `storefront` (`get`, `update`), `pages` (`list`, `create`, `get`, `update`, `delete`), `assets` (`list`, `upload`, `delete`) and `templates` (`list`, `get`, `put`, `preview`, `reset`); `console.payments` (`list`, `get`, `capture`, `refund`); and `storefront.listPages` and `storefront.getPage`, each named by its `x-sdk-method` and sent through the route table. The console additions send `cache: "no-store"` and the client's `X-Velexis-Environment` like the rest of `console`; the page methods are public and leave the cache mode to the caller, with `locale` as a query parameter. The updates and deletes of markup rules, fee rules, storefront settings and pages and `payments.capture` require `ifMatch` (a version number is sent as `If-Match: "<version>"`, an ETag string as it is; a missing or malformed one is a `TypeError` that names whose version it is, before any request); `templates.put` sends `If-Match` only when `ifMatch` is given. `assets.upload` sends `multipart/form-data` from a `Blob` or `File` with the parts `kind`, `fileName` (when given) and `file` and a generated `Idempotency-Key`, leaves the `Content-Type` and its boundary to `fetch`, and refuses a `file` that is not a `Blob` or a missing `kind` with a `TypeError`. The settings and payments lists resolve with a `Page`. `console.js` exports the parameter, schema and namespace types of the additions (`ConsoleListMarkupRulesParams` … `ConsolePaymentsApi`) after the contract's names. The parity test's pending list is empty: the client has every `x-sdk-method` of the document. The ESM bundle is 14.6 KB min+gzip.
- The `auth` and `account` namespaces (SDK-008): `auth.signUp`, `.verifyEmail`, `.resendVerification`, `.signIn`, `.refresh`, `.signOut`, `.requestPasswordReset`, `.resetPassword`, `.changePassword` and `.getSession`; `account.get`, `.update`, `.delete` and the deferred `account.preferences.*`, `account.savedTravelers.*` and `account.dataExports.*` (nine methods: `ServerError` `NOT_IMPLEMENTED`), each named by its `x-sdk-method` and sent through the route table; `account.savedTravelers.list` resolves with a `Page`; every method sends `cache: "no-store"` unless the call sets `cache` or `next`. `account.update`, `account.preferences.update` and `account.savedTravelers.update` require `ifMatch` (a version number is sent as `If-Match: "<version>"`, an ETag string as it is; a missing or malformed one is a `TypeError` before any request). `auth.signIn`, `auth.refresh` and `auth.changePassword` are typed `AuthSessionResult | AuthSessionInfo`, the approved deviation of OADR-0006. In token and server mode their results feed the client's session: `onSessionRefreshed` gets `result.session`, and the client sends the new tokens wherever its token sources still give the replaced ones, so it never presents a rotated refresh token again; a client without token options only reports them, a call with its own `options.accessToken` or `Authorization` header feeds nothing, and a staff client keeps its staff session. Cookie mode stores nothing: `auth.refresh` sends an empty body, a `refreshToken` in the params of `refresh` or `signOut` is a `TypeError` before any request, the session results resolve without token fields and `signUp` and `resetPassword` with `session: null`, whatever a response says. The public module `identity.js` with the auth and account typedefs of `identity.d.ts` and the factories `createAuthApi` and `createAccountApi`. The ESM bundle is 13.7 KB min+gzip.
- The `console` namespace with the 32 methods of the contract (SDK-011): `auth` (`signIn`, `refresh`, `signOut`, `me`), `bookings` (`list`, `get`, `findByRecordLocator`, `import`, `retrieve`, `patch`, `issue`, `cancel`, `resync`, `addNote` and the deferred `create`), `queues` (`list`, `listSnapshots`), `customers` (`list`, `get`), `supplierRequests.list` and the raw Amadeus pass-through `amadeus` (`searchFlightOffers`, `getFlightOffers`, `searchFlightAvailabilities`, `upsellAirOffers`, `quoteAirOffers`, `getFlightOrder`, `getFlightOrderByReference`, `getQueue`, and the deferred writes `createFlightOrders`, `issueFlightOrder`, `patchFlightOrder`, `cancelFlightOrder`), each named by its `x-sdk-method` and sent through the route table (60 s for `bookings.issue`, `.cancel`, `.import` and `.retrieve`); the five deferred methods surface `501 NOT_IMPLEMENTED` as a `ServerError` that is not retryable; the five lists resolve with a `Page` that keeps `totalCount`, `snapshot` and `warnings`; every method sends `cache: "no-store"` unless the call sets `cache` or `next`, and with the client option `environment` every console request carries `X-Velexis-Environment` (customer requests never do). `bookings.patch`, `.issue` and `.cancel` require `ifMatch` (a version number is sent as `If-Match: "<version>"`, an ETag string as it is; a missing or malformed one is a `TypeError` before any request); `bookings.findByRecordLocator` and `amadeus.getFlightOrderByReference` send `originSystemCode: "GDS"` when the call leaves it out. The public module `console.js` with the typedefs of `console.d.ts` (the `sessionKind` and `onStaffSessionRefreshed` augmentation stays in `shared.js`) and the factory `createConsoleApi`. `console` stays in the parity test's pending list until SDK-012 adds `console.settings` and `console.payments`; the contract project checks its 32 contract methods. The ESM bundle is 13.0 KB min+gzip.
- The `checkout` namespace (SDK-009): `create`, `get`, `setTravelers`, `setContacts`, `setAncillaries`, `setRemarks` (the steps with an optional `ifMatch`), `createPayment`, `updatePayment`, `getPayment`, `complete` (90 s, retried with the same `Idempotency-Key`) and the MVP addition `completeWithAirlineCard` (hosted storefront only: 90 s, a new key per call, never retried, and its errors rebuilt without anything of the card, in their texts, fields or causes); every `checkout.*` call sends `cache: "no-store"` unless it sets `cache` or `next`. `ifMatch` given as a number (the resource `version`) is now sent as its ETag, `If-Match: "<n>"`, as the contract's `IfMatchValue` says; a string is sent as it is. The public module `bookings.js` with the schema aliases and the checkout typedefs of `bookings.d.ts`, `CheckoutApi`, the addition types `AirlineCard` and `CompleteWithAirlineCardParams`, and the factory `createCheckoutApi`. The ESM bundle is 12.2 KB min+gzip.
- The `bookings` namespace (SDK-010): `list`, `get`, `lookup`, `update`, `getAncillaries`, `createPayment` and `addAncillaries` (the last three deferred: `ServerError` `NOT_IMPLEMENTED`), `quoteCancellation`, `cancel`, `getFareRules`, `listDocuments`, `getDocumentDownloadUrl`, `listEvents` and `getTicketingStatus`, each named by its `x-sdk-method` and sent through the route table; the three lists resolve with a `Page` that iterates across cursors; every method sends `cache: "no-store"` unless the call sets `cache` or `next`. `update`, `addAncillaries` and `cancel` require `ifMatch` (a version number is sent as `If-Match: "<version>"`, an ETag string as it is; a missing or malformed one is a `TypeError` before any request). A guest booking token goes per call as `options.bookingToken` in token and server mode and is refused with a `TypeError` in cookie mode, where `lookup` resolves with `bookingToken: null`. The public module `bookings.js` with the typedefs of `bookings.d.ts` (the bookings parameters and `BookingsApi`, after the checkout typedefs of SDK-009) and the factory `createBookingsApi`. The ESM bundle is 12.5 KB min+gzip.
- The bundles (SDK-007): `pnpm --filter @velexis/ota-sdk build` writes `dist/velexis-sdk.mjs` (ESM) and `dist/velexis-sdk.iife.js` (IIFE defining `window.Velexis`), both ES2022, minified, self-contained and with linked source maps, and the declarations in `dist/types/`, then fails when the ESM bundle exceeds 25 KB min+gzip (11.3 KB now). `package.json` `exports` `"."` → `types`, `development` (`src/index.js`, for Vite's dev server and Vitest), `import` (the ESM bundle) and `default` (the same bundle, so CommonJS code can `require()` it on Node 22), and `"./iife"`; `files` lists `dist`, `src`, `README.md` and `CHANGELOG.md`. Importing either bundle performs no request and changes no global except the IIFE's `window.Velexis`.
- The M2 namespaces (SDK-005): `storefront` (`get`, and `resolve`, deferred: `ServerError` `NOT_IMPLEMENTED`), `reference` (`locations.search`, `airports.get`, `airlines.list`, `airlines.get`, `countries.list`, `currencies.list`, the lists as `Page`s) and `flights` (`search`, `getSearch`, `listResults` with the page's facets, `getOffer`, `upsell`, `availability`, deferred, `price`, `getPricedOffer`, `getAncillaries`, `getFareRules`), each named by its `x-sdk-method` and sent through the route table (60 s for `search`, `upsell` and `price`); `flights.*` send `cache: "no-store"` unless the call sets `cache` or `next`; `storefront.get({ locale })` as a shortcut for `options.locale`. The public modules `identity.js` (storefront and reference part) and `flights.js` with the typedefs of their contract files and the factories `createStorefrontApi`, `createReferenceApi` and `createFlightsApi`.
- The session modes (SDK-004): cookie mode (`session: "cookie"`: same-origin `fetch` with cookies, `/api` by default, keys, tokens and credential headers refused with a `TypeError`, `X-CSRF-Token` from `__Host-vx_csrf` with one `GET /v1/storefront` when it is missing or refused, refresh through `POST /v1/auth/refresh` with an empty body), token mode (`credentials: "omit"`) and server mode (`secretKey` refused in a browser page, `environment` → `X-Velexis-Environment` on console operations); the automatic refresh on `401 TOKEN_EXPIRED` with one refresh for concurrent calls, `sessionKind: "staff"` through `console.auth.refresh` with `onStaffSessionRefreshed`, `onSessionExpired` when the refresh is refused, and the rotated tokens kept by the client.
- The request core (SDK-003): `createVelexisClient` with option validation and the contract's defaults, `withOptions` and the `request` escape hatch; headers (`Accept-Language`, `X-Currency`, `X-Velexis-Host`, `X-Velexis-End-User-*`, the API key and tokens, caller headers), one `Idempotency-Key` per call reused across its retries, timeouts linked to `options.signal`, retries with back-off and `Retry-After`, the `onResponse` hook; the contract's error classes (`VelexisError` … `ServerError`) with `isVelexisError` and a `toJSON()` without keys or tokens; `Page<T>` with `next()` and `for await` across pages. The namespaces follow from SDK-005.
- The package scaffold (SDK-001): an ES module package with `"sideEffects": false` and no runtime dependencies; `VERSION`, the only export so far; the base type aliases `Schemas`, `Paths` and `Operations` from `@velexis/api-types` (JSDoc types only); `tsc --checkJs` with `strict`, `noUncheckedIndexedAccess`, `exactOptionalPropertyTypes` and no Node globals; declaration-only emit to `dist/types/`; Vitest with a fake `fetch` for the unit tests.
