# OADR-0011: Console access: staff sessions, environments and permissions

**Status:** Accepted
**Date:** 2026-10-12
**Deciders:** OTA backend tech lead. Decided during planning; revisit when third-party consoles need the password grant, when admin sub-roles are requested, or when raw Amadeus writes are needed by support.
**Revisions:** 2026-10-12 (planning review): point 4 uses the "direct loopback, not through Caddy" test of OADR-0009; points 8 and 9 use the staff member's forwarded IP and user agent instead of the admin server's. 2026-10-21 (lead decision, recording OBE-059): point 3 names the fourth revocation key, the membership cut-off `staffdeny:member:<staffId>` (epoch ms, 12 h TTL) that `console.auth.signOut` with `scope: all` writes and the staff guard and `console.auth.refresh` check, so that signing out every session of a staff member needs no Redis scan; the Consequences and Alternatives say why. 2026-10-21 (lead decision, second batch): point 8 is narrowed from every console call to every state-changing console call and every refused console call; successful reads and the staff-session calls `console.auth.refresh`, `console.auth.signOut` and `console.auth.me` write no row. A refusal by request validation (`400 VALIDATION_FAILED`) is a refused call too, recorded with the action and the target only; the Consequences, Follow-ups and Alternatives follow. 2026-10-21 (lead decision, third batch; FIX-W25-AUDIT): point 8 takes its final form, recorded in its dated note. The successful reads that return personal data of one person or a supplier record (viewing a customer, the PNR lookup, the order retrieve, the raw Amadeus reads) write a row, opted in per operation by its handler; every other successful read writes none, and the audit interceptor no longer writes one for it. A `429 RATE_LIMITED` writes no row but a log line; the staff guard's and the sign-in's `403` refusals of a staff member they know write a denied row; a refusal before request validation records a path value as the target only when the value passes that parameter's schema. The two point 8 Follow-ups are resolved, and the Consequences and Alternatives follow.

## Context

[ADR-0008](../../../docs/mvp/adr/0008-identity-accounts-customers-sessions.md) and [PADR-0007](../../../platform/docs/adr/0007-portal-to-ota-admin-sso-token.md) fix the SSO: a one-time `ptk_` token in Redis, redeemed with `GETDEL` by `console.auth.signIn` (`grantType = portal_token`) through the OTA admin's service key; staff refresh families live in Redis; the platform writes `staffdeny:acct:<accountId>` and requires families of at most 30 days. The OTA admin picks test or live per request, and "live requires a role that allows it". The contract's `StaffPermission` covers bookings, customers, queues, supplier requests and Amadeus, but not the settings and payments the OTA admin needs in M4. The contract also offers raw Amadeus writes and a password grant.

## Decision

1. **Grants.** Only `portal_token` in the MVP. The `password` grant answers `403 FEATURE_DISABLED` (`details.feature = "passwordGrant"`).
2. **Staff sessions.** Access JWT 15 minutes (`typ: staff`, `sub: stf_…`, `tid`, `roles`, `sid`); refresh token rotating in a Redis family `staffsess:<familyId>` with an **absolute lifetime of 12 hours** (a working day; re-entry through the portal is one click). Reuse of a rotated token revokes the family.
3. **Revocation checks** on every console request (the staff guard) and on every refresh (`console.auth.refresh`), against four revocation keys and the membership:
   - the family `staffsess:<familyId>` exists, belongs to the request's tenant, has not reached its absolute end, and is not in **`staffdeny:<familyId>`** (one family revoked: its sign-out, the reuse of a rotated refresh token, a refused refresh);
   - its creation time is after three epoch-ms cut-offs: **`staffdeny:acct:<accountId>`** (written by the platform, [PADR-0007](../../../platform/docs/adr/0007-portal-to-ota-admin-sso-token.md); 30 days), **`staffdeny:tenant:<tenantId>`** (written on `tenant.suspend`, [OADR-0007](0007-tenant-migration-runner-and-provisioning-worker.md) point 8; 30 days) and **`staffdeny:member:<staffId>`** (written by `console.auth.signOut` with `scope: all`: the current time in epoch ms, with a TTL of 12 hours, the absolute lifetime of a family, so when it expires every family it refused has ended too). A family created at or before a cut-off is refused, and a cut-off that is not a whole number of milliseconds refuses every family. A membership (`stf_…`) belongs to one tenant, so the member cut-off ends that staff member's sessions in this OTA only, and a later sign-in through the portal opens a family that passes it;
   - the cached membership is `active`, of an active account, and its role is the family's role, which is the token's role (a role change forces a new sign-in within 60 s through the `membership` invalidation).

   `console.auth.signOut` with `scope: all` revokes its own family as well and needs the access token of a live session or the family's current refresh token. The family, its deny key and the tenant cut-off are read in one `MGET`, then the account and member cut-offs the family names in a second; a Redis that cannot answer refuses the request (`503 SERVICE_UNAVAILABLE`), because a revocation that cannot be checked is never skipped.
4. **Service key rules.** Accepted only on a direct loopback connection that did not come through Caddy (no `X-Forwarded-For` or `X-Forwarded-Host`, [OADR-0009](0009-caller-and-tenant-resolution.md) rule 1.2), only on console operations, only with a staff token whose `tid` is the tenant (except the sign-in itself). Calls from a custom console use a secret key scoped `console` with a staff token, or `console:automation` keys with explicit permissions, as the contract defines.
5. **Environments.** The OTA admin sends `X-Velexis-Environment`. `live` requires the tenant's `storefront_environment = live` (an active live Amadeus connection exists by then) and a role in `owner`, `admin`, `agent`, `finance`; `viewer` is limited to test. Otherwise `403 ENVIRONMENT_NOT_ALLOWED`. `ConsoleStaffSession.environments` tells the admin which to offer.
6. **New permissions** `settings:read`, `settings:write`, `payments:read`, `payments:capture`, `payments:refund` with the role matrix of [`../scope.md`](../scope.md) §1.3. Settings writes are owner and admin only, because markups, fees and templates change revenue and customer communication.
7. **Raw Amadeus access is read-only** in the MVP: the eight read operations (`amadeus:read`) are built; the four writes (`createFlightOrders`, `issueFlightOrder`, `patchFlightOrder`, `cancelFlightOrder`) answer `501 NOT_IMPLEMENTED`. Bookings are changed through `console.bookings.*`, which keeps payments, tickets, events and documents consistent.
8. **Audit.** Every state-changing console call, every refused console call, and every successful console read that returns personal data of one person or a supplier record writes one `staff_actions` row with actor, role, action, target, reason, redacted before/after, request id, IP and user agent. For the OTA admin these are the staff member's: the admin forwards `X-Velexis-End-User-IP` (the right-most `X-Forwarded-For` entry set by Caddy) and `X-Velexis-End-User-Agent` on every SDK call, and the backend honours them only for the service caller ([OADR-0009](0009-caller-and-tenant-resolution.md) point 8). Without them every row would show `127.0.0.1`.
   - **Changes** are written by their use case in the transaction of the change (`outcome = succeeded`); a change that fails is recorded as `failed` in a transaction of its own, since the failure rolls back the use case's row. An idempotent replay of a change keeps its `succeeded` row. `console.auth.signIn` writes its `staff.sign_in` row.
   - **Refusals** are written in a transaction of their own: by the permission guard (`403 FORBIDDEN`, `ENVIRONMENT_NOT_ALLOWED`, `SCOPE_NOT_ALLOWED`: `denied`), by the staff guard (`403 STAFF_MEMBERSHIP_INACTIVE` of a verified staff token: `denied`, with the token's staff member and role as the actor), by `console.auth.signIn` (its `403 STAFF_MEMBERSHIP_INACTIVE`, `TENANT_SUSPENDED`, `MFA_REQUIRED` and `ENVIRONMENT_NOT_ALLOWED` of the staff member its portal token names: a `denied` `staff.sign_in`), by request validation and the idempotency check (`400 VALIDATION_FAILED`, `409 IDEMPOTENCY_KEY_REUSED`: `failed`, with the action and the target only, never the request's body, query or headers, and no target that validation refused) and by the use case (`denied` for a permission or office refusal, `failed` otherwise). The guards run before request validation, so their rows take a path value as the target only after it passes that parameter's schema in the OpenAPI document; otherwise the target is empty.
   - **Audited reads**: viewing a customer (`customer.view`), the PNR lookup (`pnr.lookup`), the order retrieve (`order.retrieve`) and the eight raw Amadeus reads (`amadeus.<operationId>`), written after the read in a transaction of its own. Each opts in explicitly, per operation, where it is implemented (the handler's `@AuditedRead`, [`../../backend/README.md`](../../backend/README.md)); a read that has not opted in writes no row.
   - **No row** for any other successful read (lists such as bookings, queues and the customer search; a booking's detail; the supplier log; settings), for the staff-session calls `console.auth.refresh`, `console.auth.signOut` and `console.auth.me` (a guard's refusal of one is a refused call), for a request refused before its staff member or key is known (no, unknown, expired or revoked credential, a missing header), and for a `429 RATE_LIMITED`: a row needs an actor (`staff_actions_actor_ck`), and the rate limits run before the permission guard, so that a client asking again and again is throttled before it fills the table. The request's log line has the rest; a `429` also logs `rate_limit.exceeded` with the staff member or key, the operation and the limit, and nothing sensitive.
   - *Note (2026-10-21, lead decision, third batch).* This is the final rule of point 8: rows for state-changing console calls, for refused console calls of a known staff member or key, and for the successful reads that return personal data of one person or a supplier record (opted in per operation); no row for the other reads, and only a log line for a `429`; a denied call's path value becomes its target only after it passes the parameter's validation.
9. **Rate limits**: 600 requests per minute per staff session or key; `console.auth.signIn` 10 per minute per end-user IP (the forwarded one for the service caller), so the sign-ins of all OTAs, which all arrive from the admin server, never share one bucket that a single client could exhaust.

## Consequences

### Positive

- A removed or demoted member, a suspended tenant, or "sign out everywhere" takes effect within seconds and at most 60 s.
- A staff member's "sign out of every session" in the console is one key write, effective on the next console request or refresh of each other session, without knowing their family ids.
- Settings and payments get explicit permissions instead of piggybacking on booking permissions.
- No console path can create an Amadeus order that the OTA's books do not know about.
- `staff_actions` holds what staff changed, what they were refused (including requests that never passed validation) and whose personal data or which supplier record they looked at, without a row for each of the list and detail reads the OTA admin makes on every page.

### Negative

- Staff sessions are lost if Redis loses data; staff click "Open console" again.
- Viewers cannot look at live data, which some OTAs may want for accountants (they can use `finance`).
- Support staff needing raw writes must use the Amadeus terminal until the writes are built.
- A successful read other than the audited ones leaves no `staff_actions` row (who opened a booking's detail or listed customers is not recorded), and a `429` is in the logs only.
- The staff guard's refusal of an inactive membership runs before the rate limits (the console limit counts a staff session, which the refused request does not have), so a removed member's access token, valid for at most 15 minutes, writes a row for each refused request.

### Follow-ups

- Password grant and TOTP for third-party consoles, when requested.
- Raw Amadeus writes with explicit audit and a booking import afterwards, when support needs them.
- Point 8 (2026-10-21, third batch): `console.auth.refresh`'s `403 STAFF_MEMBERSHIP_INACTIVE` and the tenant guard's `403 TENANT_SUSPENDED` of a known key are not among the refusals the decision named, and keep their log line only; whether point 8 asks for rows for them is for the lead to decide.

## Alternatives considered

- **30-day staff families like the portal.** The OTA admin handles payments and personal data; a shorter session limits the damage of a stolen cookie.
- **Environment from a separate service key per environment.** Two keys for one process, and the choice belongs to the staff member, not the deployment.
- **Reuse `bookings:write` for settings.** Agents would then change markups.
- **Audited reads named in the OpenAPI document** (an `x-audit` extension that the interceptor reads). The handler that returns the personal data is the code that knows it does; a decorator keeps the opt-in next to it, needs no generator or extension-test change, and leaves the document's descriptions to say which reads are audited.
- **A row for every console call** (point 8 until 2026-10-21, as OBE-060 built it). Most rows would be reads that change nothing, among which the changes and refusals an audit is read for are hard to find; the OTA backend's document already says of its bookings list "not audited per call" and of its booking detail "Viewing is not audited" (`consoleListBookings`, `consoleGetBooking`).
- **Sign out every session of a member by finding their families** (a `SCAN` over `staffsess:*`, or a per-member set of family ids kept beside the families). A scan walks every staff family of every OTA on the shared Redis for one sign-out; a set is a second structure that must follow the families' creation, rotation, revocation and expiry. A cut-off is one write, read in the same `MGET` as the account cut-off, and expires on its own.
