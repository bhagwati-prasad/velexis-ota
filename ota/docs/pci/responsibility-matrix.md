# PCI evidence: responsibility matrix

Who is responsible for each PCI DSS v4.0.1 requirement that applies to the airline-card path, which Velexis validates with the **SAQ D for Service Providers** ([ADR-0011](../../../docs/mvp/adr/0011-payments-stripe-and-airline-card.md) §C): Velexis, the OTA (Velexis's customer), or a third-party service provider. It is the matrix that requirements **12.8.5** (for the OTAs, which use Velexis as a service provider) and **12.9.2** (for Velexis, which gives it to them) ask for, and it references the controls of [ADR-0027](../../../docs/mvp/adr/0027-pci-scope-and-vm-controls.md) row by row. It is part of the [PCI evidence pack](README.md).

References follow the convention of [`data-flow.md`](data-flow.md): a link named `file:line` and, after it, a piece of that line in code style.

## 1. How to read it

- **Velexis** runs the whole cardholder data environment: the VM (the storefront pages, Caddy, the APIs and workers, the admin apps, PostgreSQL and Redis), and the systems that can change it (the GitHub repository and its deploy job, the developer's SSH path and workstation, the backup bucket, the cloud, DNS and SMTP accounts). Every requirement on those is Velexis's.
- **The OTA** never runs code on the VM and never sees a card: its storefront is Velexis's build, its console shows a card's brand, BIN and last 4 only. Its part is its own people, accounts, domain and channels, and its own validation as a merchant or agent.
- **Third parties** run the systems around the VM (§3); Velexis manages them under 12.8.
- **Status** is the state of Velexis's control in this repository: *in place*, *open* (with the task), or *outside the repository* (a document, an account setting or a record that lives elsewhere and must exist before the SAQ).
- **Not applicable** is written where the requirement has no object here, with the reason.

## 3. Third-party service providers (12.8)

For each provider Velexis keeps a written agreement that says what it is responsible for (12.8.2), checks it before engaging it (12.8.3), checks its PCI DSS compliance at least once a year where it touches card data or can affect the CDE (12.8.4: its Attestation of Compliance, AOC, or the evidence it offers), and keeps the split of requirements below (12.8.5). The agreements and AOCs are kept outside the repository; their dates go into the [calendar](README.md#3-calendar).

| Provider | What it does for the card path | Card data | Its responsibility | Velexis's responsibility |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Amadeus** (Enterprise APIs, Flight Create Orders) | Receives the card in `formOfPayments` and passes it to the validating airline | The whole card, in transit and in its own systems | PCI DSS for its own systems and the onward transmission to the airline | Send the card over HTTPS only and nothing else of it ([`data-flow.md`](data-flow.md) §3, TLS); check Amadeus's AOC yearly |
| **The validating airline** | Charges the card (the merchant of the airline-card payment) | The whole card | PCI DSS as a merchant, its acquirer relationship | None directly: Velexis has no agreement with the airline; reached through Amadeus |
| **Stripe** (method (a)) | Stripe.js and the Payment Element on the card page, card processing | The whole card, in Stripe's iframes and systems only | PCI DSS for Stripe.js, its iframes and its processing | Serve Stripe.js on the card page only and check the page daily ([`script-inventory.md`](script-inventory.md)); check Stripe's AOC yearly |
| **The cloud provider** | The VM, its disk, network and console | Hosts the CDE (no card data at rest) | Physical security (9), the hypervisor and the network under the VM | MFA on the console (8.4); check its AOC or equivalent yearly |
| **The DNS provider** | The records of every Velexis host | None, but it can redirect the storefront hosts | Its own systems | MFA on the account (8.4); OTAs keep their custom domains' DNS safe (§2, row 8) |
| **Let's Encrypt** (through Caddy) | Certificates of every host | None | The CA | Valid certificates and their expiry check (4.2.1) |
| **The off-site backup bucket's provider** | Encrypted backups with object lock | None (backups hold no card data) | Storage and object lock | Encryption to off-VM keys, a VM key that cannot delete (X-024), MFA on the account |
| **The SMTP provider** | Alerts and transactional e-mail | None | Its own systems | MFA on the account; never put card data into an e-mail |
| **GitHub** | The repository, Actions (build and deploy), Dependabot | None, but the deploy job can change the VM | Its own systems | Branch and environment protection, actions pinned by SHA, MFA (6.5, 8.4; ADR-0023) |

## 4. What Velexis gives the OTAs (12.9)

- **A written acknowledgement** (12.9.1), in the agreement with each OTA that enables the airline card, that Velexis is responsible for the security of the card data it receives, transmits and processes on the OTA's behalf, and for the requirements this matrix gives to Velexis. The agreement is outside the repository; the M6 review (OBE-086) checks that it exists before the first OTA turns the airline card on for live payments.
- **On request** (12.9.2): Velexis's PCI DSS compliance status (its AOC once the SAQ D is complete), this matrix, and a summary of the latest scans and penetration test ([README §4](README.md#4-scan-test-and-incident-records)).
- **In an incident**: the notifications of [`incident-response.md`](incident-response.md) §5.
